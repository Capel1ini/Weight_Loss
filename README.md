This is a Weight_Loss app that streamlines your weight loss journey by making it easy to monitor your calories, exercise, logging meals etc. This software doesn't make recommendations but 
essentially gives you a projection based on the data you provide. For it to work optimally, it needs at least a week of logging to give you accurate data. 

To give an outline of the app: 

You can log each meal you ate, including all the macros. It also allows you to take a pic of the nutrition label to fill all the details 
You can make templates for repetitive meals so that you don't have to relog the same meals constantly. 
It allows you to enter your weight, height, sex, age to determine your tdee then set your weight goal and when you want to reach it 
It shows a timeline of your weight fluctuations
